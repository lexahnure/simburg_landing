OTA Platform

Over-the-air card management at campaign scale — remote SIM and eSIM management for telecom and IoT use cases.

Secure Remote Management

Manage files, parameters, keys, and applets across a live installed base. Secured via ETSI and 3GPP standards utilizing KIc/KID cryptographic keysets, counter-based replay protection, and precise proof-of-receipt (PoR).

Advanced Delivery Channels

Engineered for SCP80 (binary SMS) and SCP81 (high-bandwidth operations where the card opens a PSK-TLS admin session per GlobalPlatform Amendment B).

Enterprise Campaign Layer

Built for large-scale operations with cohort targeting, scheduling, and intelligent throttling aligned with SMSC capacity. Features automated per-card retry policies driven by PoR status words, resumable long scripts, and comprehensive APDU audit trails.
